Add instruments whose names are missing or unreadable without leaving the score.

## What's new

- In **Select Instrument Names on Score**, drag a box in the blank space beside an unnamed staff. If no name can be read, the box stays highlighted in orange and a focused name field appears below the score.
- Type the instrument name, choose its staff count, then press **Add Instrument** or Return. The box turns green and you can select the next instrument.
- Separate selections with the same name become separate numbered parts, just like printed-name selections. Choose one staff for each individual instrument or two for a piano grand staff.
- **Cancel** or Escape discards the pending entry. **Done — Back to Auto Extract** is available after adding or canceling it, so typed text is not silently discarded.
- Navigation and source changes invalidate unfinished selections; accepted names remain in the setup list. Selection boxes identify names for setup and do not become staff crops.

All Alpha 5 improvements, including clarinet and grand-staff rest detection and default joining of consecutive silent systems, are included.

## Download and open

Download **Partsmith-v0.1.0-alpha.6-macos.zip** below, unzip it, and open `Partsmith.app`. Requires **macOS 14 or later**, on Apple Silicon or Intel. The app is ad hoc signed, not notarized; if macOS blocks it, use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** after attempting to launch it.

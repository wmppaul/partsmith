# Sample score set for Partsmith MVP

These are deliberately **normal, clean, non-edge-case** PDFs for the first development folder.

## Notes
- For the Mutopia entries below, prefer the **A4 PDF** download from the piece page.
- For the CPDL entries below, use the **specific edition numbers** called out.
- I intentionally left out skewed scans, photocopies, rotated pages, and malformed PDFs from this first folder.

## 01_chamber
1. Mozart — String Quartet KV 387  
   Category: born-digital chamber score  
   Why: fixed 4-staff systems; excellent baseline for band assignment and propagation  
   Source page: https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=245

2. Mozart — Piano Quartet KV 478  
   Category: born-digital chamber score  
   Why: mixed layout with piano grand staff plus 3 string staves  
   Source page: https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=499

3. Mozart — Trio in E-flat Major KV 498  
   Category: born-digital chamber score  
   Why: clarinet/violin + viola + piano; another mixed-height chamber layout  
   Source page: https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=250

## 02_orchestra
4. Mozart — Symphony no. 18 in F major, KV 130  
   Category: orchestral score with many staves  
   Why: compact classical orchestra; useful first orchestral benchmark  
   Source page: https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=346

5. Beethoven — Overture to Egmont, Op. 84  
   Category: orchestral score with many staves  
   Why: standard classical orchestra with winds, brass, timpani, and strings  
   Source page: https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=547

6. Mozart — Die Zauberflöte (The Magic Flute) – Ouverture, KV 620  
   Category: orchestral score with many staves  
   Why: medium-size full orchestra including trombones; clean full-score case

## 04_choir
9. Mozart — Ave verum corpus, KV 618  
   Category: choir score  
   Recommended edition: **CPDL #18715** (complete score, 4 pages)  
   Why: very common SATB test piece with accompaniment options and keyboard reduction  
   Source page: https://www.cpdl.org/wiki/index.php/Ave_verum_corpus_%28Wolfgang_Amadeus_Mozart%29

10. Mendelssohn — Verleih uns Frieden gnädiglich  
    Category: choir score  
    Recommended edition: **CPDL #63797** (full score, SATB choir with organ accompaniment, A4, 6 pages)  
    Why: SATB with organ accompaniment and vocal-score options; good choral benchmark  
    Source page: https://www.cpdl.org/wiki/index.php/Verleih_uns_Frieden_gn%C3%A4diglich_%28Felix_Mendelssohn%29

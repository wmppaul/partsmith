The 57 unchanged source-region checks retain 54 passes and three failures: the page-35 Violin I broad rectangle and the page-22 Cello lower rectangle/right landmark. These are not waived or fitted to the candidate. Complete Vivace source ink is retained by its unchanged crop. A guard check covers only its recorded rectangle, and visual observation is not an exhaustive pixel ownership oracle.

This candidate remains **rejected for promotion**. The [independent source-mask controls](../combined-span-v4-independent-2026-10-03/README.md) preserve every one of 891 owner masks, recovering 88,821 previously omitted pixel observations, but all 27 local-note/barline junction cases acquire false shared ownership and 84 additional whole-neighbor relations. Real-score improvement does not erase that separately recorded regression. True filled noteheads touching a barline do not establish common ownership of the line or the other heads.

The replay ran after the preservation checks, as a diagnostic examination of a known separation failure. It took 11.83 seconds for native analysis and planning of the frozen 39 rasters; PDF rendering, compilation and visual review are outside that timing. The higher-resolution corrected-page experiment is not included. No full corpus run, candidate export, app worker, production change or app replacement follows from this result.

Genuine heads may share staff/ledger fragments or carry long ties. That rejected rule was not rerun or reintroduced here.

## What the planner can and cannot recover

The current component record stores only bounds, staff IDs and the alternative flag. It cannot distinguish a shared musical stem from a structural spine with a local slur when their boxes and owner sets coincide. Accordingly, a planner-only exception cannot be source justified. The four-component ablation in this folder is **causal diagnosis only**, never a crop proposal: it deletes music-bearing evidence. It changes eight crop rectangles, including p31 Violin I, and one additional warning. P38's ablated Cello top is 119.959pt, still much broader than the source-reviewed 131.5pt manual edge; rejecting that alternative alone does not reproduce the clean manual crop.

A falsifiable next mechanism is a source-preserving, typed spine/branch graph: establish the physical measure/system boundary independently, retain actual local branches and their staff ownership after staff-line intersections, and remove only structural connectivity from attribution. It must explicitly preserve true filled/hollow/tied heads and shared musical stems. Acceptance requires the frozen seven envelopes and thirteen local obligations, no new or worsened losses in all existing controls, and no added whole-neighbor cases in native source replay. At present neither the component metadata nor a compactness cutoff provides that proof. No new classifier is proposed for promotion.

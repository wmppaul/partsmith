# Literal scale and spacing, with source-aligned strips

The width recommendation no longer clamps Scale. Requested 1.30× and 1.40× produce different sizes; the Inspector warns when retained music may cross the margins or be cut off at the physical paper edge. The existing 0.6–1.4 slider range remains. One crop taller than a whole output page still fits vertically. Bar numbers move to a separate row when oversized music reaches their former margin position.

System Gap is now the actual distance used by pagination, including when Balance Page Fill is on. Balancing distributes complete systems without reducing the chosen gap. Larger gaps can add pages. The 4–200-point range, 18-point new-project side margins and all saved source crops remain intact.

Consistent-scale strips share a source-coordinate reference instead of each trimmed crop being centered independently. In the exact 39-page Brahms source, a right-edge speck in section A's crop previously introduced a 19.70-point left shift at the same 1.05× scale. The corrected mapping removes that false shift. Instrument-name picking does not write crop geometry or introduce indents. Independent scaling retains its per-strip fit behavior; flexible generated rests retain their adjustable drawn width.

The independent [layout/export regression suite](../literal-layout-regressions-2026-10-04/README.md) passes 7,157 layout assertions and 411 native export checks. The [Brahms before/after review](../brahms-alignment-2026-10-04/README.md) adds 11 baseline and 69 candidate checks: source-aligned positions, exact 100/200-point gaps with Balance on, proportional 1.3/1.4 enlargement and seven pages pixel-identical to complete-source reference drawings. The enlarged overflow fixtures intentionally clip left-edge notation and demonstrate the warning behavior; they are not deliverable musician parts. A [340-point native Inspector review](../literal-layout-ui-2026-10-04/README.md) passes 13 checks. Root inspected the alignment comparison and final warning/spacing UI.

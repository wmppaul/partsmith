# Frozen before prototype results

This diagnostic prototype adds an independent structural continuation proof only when the current translated core/junction proof fails. It does not relax the existing88%/junction or line-tracing thresholds, change the immediate-core shortcut, or alter terminal musical-body protection and branch-aware gap removal.

A failed core must retain both outer longitudinal junctions on the single measured undilated physical stroke, plus independently sustained fragments for all five ordered staff lines in the existing-sized left/right flanks. A broad search halo is never structural continuity evidence for this fallback.

Three OTHER consecutive cores, all above or all below that failed core, must independently satisfy the existing88% support and all five longitudinal junctions in that same exact measured physical-stroke corridor. Their global alignment is tried first; an already-existing localCoreShift result may establish alignment if needed. Every row of every intervening inter-staff gap must have original source ink inside that same physical corridor. An uninterrupted source vertical group is required; staff lists from separate systems are not concatenated across whitespace. No sideways stepping, dilation or jump to a neighboring stem is permitted.

This is an extra barline-context hypothesis, not a notehead classifier. A real multi-core musical spine can potentially share structural geometry. Existing musical ownership and adversarial controls remain mandatory and cannot be weakened. The native source result, original manual targets and all source guards remain independent acceptance checks. A failure ends this V1 experiment; no tuning against the observed outcome.

# Brahms Op.67 full-source review

Source: `sample_scores/medium_skewed/05_brahms_string_quartet_no3_op67_imslp_09200.pdf`.

This audit covers every printed system on all 25 PDF pages: 120 systems and 480 physical staves. Printed order is 1. Violine, 2. Violine, Bratsche, Violoncell(o). All four parts appear in every system. All four movements, the third-movement Trio and Coda, repeat endings, and the Da Capo instruction are included. Source page counts, part identity and movement starts were established independently from full-page source contact sheets before native detection was accepted.

The complete machine-readable source map is `brahms-quartet-map.json`; each page carries a specific review observation, and each part band has a physical protected region. Raw source pages were inspected at 2x resolution; hard-page native overlays (3,4,7,8,12,21,22,25) were also checked against actual staff identity. The final v7 p9 first-system cello overlay was inspected separately after detector correction: the original narrow false phase was replaced by the actual 14.30-point staff span. No manually supplied detector rectangles are used.

The profile uses top padding of 11 staff spaces for Violin I, 10.25 for the other instruments, and bottom padding of 9 spaces for all. This retains high rehearsal frames G (p5s3), M (p9s1), D (p12s2), high ledger notes and slurs, and low dynamics/accents. The additional quarter staff space on the lower parts preserves the original conservative guards on p21; guards were not reduced to make a crop pass. Source trims of 16 points left and 10 points right retain all printed target notes, clefs, barlines, repeat/key changes and relevant source labels across the 25 pages. The page-specific source widths and skew margins are taken from native v7.

There are no per-band crop rectangle overrides and no exclusion masks. Neighboring staff fragments are permitted under the user's preservation policy and must not be mistaken for clipping of target notation.

Forty source fragments carry shared directions into parts where the direction was only printed on another staff. Every fragment was rendered at 4x or 5x and visually inspected. This includes the Da Capo direction below the cello on p18s3, the return cross on p17s1, alternate endings, rehearsal letters, common tempos, and the fermata above the final repeat barline on p20s5. That last fermata is isolated in source rectangle [548,631,564,641] without target note fragments. Its complete glyph was inspected in `.build/full-score-review/quartet/cue-proof/p20s5-fermata-final.png`. The original source glyph remains on Violin I and is copied into Violin II, Viola, and Cello at the same horizontal position. Directions printed independently in all parts, such as dim. ed un poco rit., stay in their normal staff crops.

Accounting: native 480/480 staff proposals; zero detector replacements; zero custom crop rectangles; zero masks; 40 manually verified source cue fragments; 24 page overrides containing cue association/movement breaks only. Final output correctness also requires the independent native export pixel and page-layout review; this source audit alone does not mark an output PDF reviewed.

# Complete Partsmith score parts

All 19 parts cover the complete five source scores. These PDFs were generated by Partsmith's native offline analyzer, planner and PDF exporter, with reviewed instrument setup and musical metadata. Ave uses 8 explicit crop corrections out of 64 bands; the Quartet uses 321 out of 480. These are reviewed outputs, not unattended Auto results. The revised Ave and Quartet remove broad neighboring staff areas; small overlapping fragments remain under the preservation policy. No whiteout masks were used.

The pages are balanced within songs/movements, with consistent notation scale, source-page breaks removed, and page-number footers. Shorter pages at a song or movement end are intentional. The original scans contain small staves; these files preserve those scans rather than re-engraving or transposing the music.

Each folder includes an editable `.partsmithproject` with its immutable source PDF embedded, a placement manifest and the reviewed plan. Open the project with the updated Partsmith app to adjust layout and re-export locally without internet access. Names/counts and crop context are saved with the project. Compact mode follows detected ink; Auto review also provides local Top/Bottom edge controls. Shared source directions were verified manually; Auto does not recognize their musical meaning.

## Review scope

The full set has 156 pages, down from 184. Ave remains eight one-page parts with larger readable staves; neighboring line-center inclusions fall from 227 to 12. The Quartet falls from 75 to 47 pages and from 3,629 to 148 neighboring line centers, with no complete extra staff retained. These metrics supplement visual source review. Ave still includes small overlapping fragments and two publisher lines; the Quartet retains fragments where vertical notation ranges overlap.

Every output page received visual review. Independent source/crop reviews cover all 1,131 part bands. Geometry validation checks complete system coverage, source order, crop/placement bounds, staff containment and source/output hashes. Fidelity reports compare independently reviewed source envelopes and shared directions with the actual PDF output. These checks do not claim automatic note recognition or universal reliability on new scores.

See `review-summary.json`, the individual `review-record.json` files and `evidence/` for exact hashes, independent findings and verification methods. The source detector was validated on 84 PDF pages and 1,357 physical music staves, with no manual staff-position replacements in these outputs. Instrument setup, local crop corrections, section breaks and shared markings remain reviewed inputs. Auto alone still needs substantial crop review on the difficult Quartet scan.

# Independent final Quartet layout review

Reviewer: root, independent of the source-map author (`score_survey`). Scope: all four complete native parts, all 120 source systems in each part, all 75 exported pages. The source-map author's separate full-source musical review is in `brahms-quartet-review.md`.

Status: **visual layout and selected vulnerable-detail review pass** for the exact PDFs below. This review accepts retained neighboring notation under the user's preservation requirement. It does not claim clean staff isolation or that contact-sheet inspection alone can prove every note.

## Complete page inspection

Every page was viewed on contact sheets rendered from the final native PDFs: Violin I pages 1–18; Violin II, Viola and Violoncello pages 1–19 each. Titles, part names, movement starts, source order, page ends, vertical separation, final cadences, and page-number footers were checked. Pages generally contain five to eight systems; source-page boundaries do not force output page breaks. Shorter endings occur at explicit musical movement boundaries. No blank, duplicate, one-system orphan or overfull page was observed. The original source's small staff size remains a readability limit; notation was not shrunk to force denser pagination.

The broad crops often include most or all of an adjacent staff and fragments from the preceding/following system. These are conspicuous, especially in the first violin and cello, and are disclosed rather than masked away. Original tempo/rehearsal fragments appear above the relevant systems in lower parts; some are also visible in the retained context, so a few directions are duplicated visually.

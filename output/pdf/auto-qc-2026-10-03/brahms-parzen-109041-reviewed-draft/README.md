Complete assisted draft of Brahms’s *Gesang der Parzen*, Op. 89, from IMSLP109041.

All 20 printed section parts are included: 26 systems per part, 520 source rows and 568 printed staves across 62 PDF pages. The Alto and Bass vocal sections retain both staves where divided. The opening eighth-note pickup and all 176 numbered bars remain in source order; no missing music or generated rests were invented.

The editable Partsmith project embeds the complete original 27-page score. Native assignment, saving, reopening and export were verified. Source grouping and bar numbers were independently reviewed. Two explicit crop repairs restore a Horn slur crest and the bottom of a Trombone “dim.” marking. There are 183 original-source direction copies; six redundant Double Bass copies were removed after confirming the complete local originals.

**This is a draft, not a clean or performance-ready set.** Seven crops still contain a complete neighboring staff, and fragments remain widely visible. Some foreign instructions are misleading: Bass-only “pizz.” appears in Cello, Cello-only “arco” appears in Viola, flute-switch labels appear in Oboes, and choir text appears in Timpani. Small notation and page turns need practical review. All 520 source crops and all 62 final rendered pages were covered by review; these observations do not certify every note or instruction.

The original three-line title is copied literally, with explicit composer attribution. Historical dedication/publication text is omitted from the part headers. The bar-116 metric relation is placed below the four choir rows to avoid their expression and lyrics; its original horizontal position is retained. Original source notation is not re-engraved or transposed.

# Brahms Clarinet Trio, Op. 114

Complete parts from the medium-skewed IMSLP 114012 scan, generated with Partsmith's native analyzer, planner, layout engine and PDF exporter:

| Part | Pages | Source systems |
| --- | ---: | ---: |

Open `Brahms Clarinet Trio Op. 114.partsmithproject` in Partsmith to edit the crops or export locally. It embeds the complete original 35-page score. Pages 34–35 are the reviewed blank leaf and publisher catalog; all music on pages 1–33 is included. Each movement starts on a new output page. Printed bar numbers and shared tempos are preserved or copied from verified source fragments.

All 524 physical staves were detected automatically after selecting the Clarinet / Cello / two-staff Piano setup. This finished set also includes 224 explicit crop adjustments: 222 trims of unnecessary neighboring context and two tiny conservative guard expansions. The other 169 crops retain Auto geometry. No notes were transcribed, transposed, erased or re-engraved, and no whiteout masks were used.

Small neighboring note, slur and text fragments remain where they share the target notation's height. The final crops contain no complete neighboring staves; four neighboring staff-line centers remain across all 393 bands. Clarinet and Cello retain the study score's relatively small staff size, approximately 4mm on Letter paper; Piano is approximately 5.8mm.

The machine review checks all source coverage, crop bounds, source guards, output placement and exact pixel fidelity in 654 protected/copy regions. Independent visual review is recorded in `review/clarinet-cello-visual.json` and `review/piano-visual.json`; final artifact hashes and review status are in `review.json`. The editable project is checked against the exported geometry and shared markings.

The source-specific crop adjustments and copied markings are separate from the general Auto improvements. Auto still requires a reviewed instrument setup and source review; it does not automatically understand every shared direction or decide ownership of all overlapping notation.

The current preview app is `artifacts/macos/Partsmith-extraction-preview-macos.zip` in the repository. Quit an older running build before opening the app from that ZIP to use the improved Auto algorithm and controls. The reviewed project can also be opened by the previous extraction preview.

# Current complete-score drafts

These are complete outputs from the native Partsmith planner and exporter, with initialized instrument lists and experimental local recognition of printed headings and repeat directions. The current Mac preview offers optional heading and repeat-direction recognition; the separately tested ending detector remains a prototype. Original source engraving is retained; OCR text is not used to replace printed music or instructions.

- **Brahms Quartet Op. 67, IMSLP93521, October 3 draft:** [Violin I](brahms-quartet-93521-traced-lines/Violin%20I.pdf), [Violin II](brahms-quartet-93521-traced-lines/Violin%20II.pdf), [Viola](brahms-quartet-93521-traced-lines/Viola.pdf), [Violoncello](brahms-quartet-93521-traced-lines/Violoncello.pdf). All 151 systems appear in each part, across 17, 16, 16 and 15 output pages. Two page-24 crops now exclude most of the neighboring staff while preserving both instruments' notation. All 42 direction-copy rows, including the eight first/second ending brackets in every part, remain unchanged. The folder includes an editable `.partsmithproject`, source-bound manifest, plan, and full corrected review source. The other 602 music crops are unchanged. Older Quartet folders remain available for comparison.
- **Mozart, Ave Verum Corpus:** all eight printed parts, eight systems each, one page per part, under [ave-verum](ave-verum/). An editable project and manifest are included.
- **Mozart, Kegelstatt Trio K.498, September 24 draft:** [Clarinet in B-flat](mozart-kv498-directions/Clarinet%20in%20B-flat.pdf), [Viola](mozart-kv498-directions/Viola.pdf), [Piano](mozart-kv498-directions/Piano.pdf). All 135 systems appear in each part, across 11, 10 and 18 pages. The complete folder includes an editable project and source-bound manifest. The printed movement headings are retained without the duplicate Clarinet Rondo heading. The source-reviewed first/second ending brackets on original page 25 now appear in all three parts. Ending detection comes from a separate experimental prototype, not the app's Auto button; main music crops remain unchanged.

**All sets remain drafts.** The latest Quartet reduces whole neighboring-staff inclusions from 20 to 9 across its 604 crops; partial fragments remain. Seven large headings, the page-28 Da Capo sentence and its page-26 destination cross are retained in all four parts. Rehearsal letters and other shared directions remain incomplete. Six frozen ending safety rectangles extend outside the measured copies by up to 2.125 points; these failures remain recorded even though independent visual review finds the intended ending ink complete. Ave retains all 64 independently reviewed own-notation envelopes, including every figured-bass row; its heading copies expose duplicate Adagio markings in Basso and Violin I, and shared source bar numbers remain incomplete. K.498 still includes substantial neighboring notation; its page turns and complete musical coverage need further review. These limitations matter when playing from the parts.

The Mac preview includes the validated crop, detector, layout, persistence and planning-speed improvements. Enable **Copy detected tempos and repeat directions (experimental)** in Auto to use the native heading/navigation/symbol workflow. Copied sources are highlighted and individual copies can be removed. The app does not yet run the paired-ending prototype used in these drafts. Use [the current Mac build](../../../artifacts/macos/Partsmith-extraction-preview-macos.zip) for editing these projects.

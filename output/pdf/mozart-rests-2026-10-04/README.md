# Mozart K.488 — opening 39 bars, rest-compression review

These nine PDFs and the editable project cover the first eight systems (bars 1–39), not the complete concerto. They use the same full embedded source and historical native Auto crops as the reproduction project, with reviewed changing instrumentation. No crop cleanup was applied.

- [Piano](Piano.pdf): one 39-bar grand-staff rest, retaining the initial clefs, signatures, brace and Allegro/TUTTI. The initial 5 printed silent bars combine with 34 confirmed omitted-staff bars.
- [Clarinet in A](Clarinet%20in%20A.pdf): the opening 5-bar rest now compresses; playing systems remain source crops.
- The other seven parts are included for comparison. Existing neighboring fragments and header/footer leakage remain in some original crops.

Six of eight musically eligible rest-only strips compress. Opening Flute and the final Clarinet strip abstain because of unrelated retained ink. Mixed systems remain original; this is not a claim of fully cleaned parts. All 72 source entries remain editable, with the original PDF unchanged. Optional first-bar numbers were omitted in this project; a second verification with numbered systems gives the same 39-bar Piano total.

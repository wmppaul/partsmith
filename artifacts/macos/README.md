# Partsmith macOS builds

Use [Partsmith-extraction-preview-macos.zip](Partsmith-extraction-preview-macos.zip) for the current extraction workflow. Unzip it and open the contained `Partsmith.app`. This is an unsigned universal arm64/x86_64 build for macOS 14 or later, not a notarized public release.

The September 27 build adds optional **Copy detected tempos and repeat directions (experimental)** in the magic-wand setup. For a consistent instrument layout, it recognizes printed headings, repeat instructions and linked destination symbols locally in the background. In Auto review, dashed purple boxes show the copied source; click a listed direction to highlight it or choose **Remove Copy** for that part. Recognition notes do not block **Add Parts**. Endings, rehearsal letters and bar numbers still need source review; the separately tested paired-ending detector is not connected to the app yet.

The integration passes 113 independent app-model checks. The real native worker was also tested on all 72 source pages of Ave, K.498 and Brahms: 1,073 crops and 42 copied directions match the CLI, and all 111 saved/reopened/exported pages are pixel-identical. Cancellation and replacement requests were tested. Live interaction with the new SwiftUI controls has not yet been verified. Build hashes and scope are recorded in [the current build record](../../Tests/quality_control/macos-build-2026-09-27-directions.json).

A separate spacing fix recovers six actual staves on La Bohème page 167 while leaving every other page's staff coordinates unchanged across all 1,477 test pages. The September 21 quality-control update separates more scanned brackets and barlines, preserves low notation while reducing spillover into the following system, and avoids repeated Auto planning. Experimental printed-direction recognition is optional and does not establish full musical coverage. This preview adds whole-score offline Auto, saved instrumentation and crop settings, reviewed assignments, mixed staff-size detection, shared source marking support, balanced pagination, output page numbers and one undoable apply. Compact cropping now follows ink, separates tilted system barlines during analysis, assigns lyric hyphens to their vocal staff, and supports validated per-band crop-edge edits during Auto review. The older `Partsmith.app` and `Partsmith-v0.1.0-alpha.2-macos.zip` in this directory are historical builds and do not include the current extraction changes. The source for rebuilding the preview lives in this repository.

The magic wand is the recommended first action after importing a score. **Auto Extract** opens a movable, resizable window. Start with optional **Deskew & Align Pages**, then choose a preset, type names, or use **Pick Names from Score** to click the printed labels. Recognition runs locally and suggests two staves for piano-like instruments. Existing page corrections are preserved; deskew is offered before bands or a source header have been selected.

Use the scrollable **Pages to extract** previews to include or leave out pages, or enter a range such as `1-8, 12` under **Selected Pages**. Deskew and Auto process only this selection. Choose **Auto** and then **Add Parts**. Pages with no detected staves are skipped automatically without an acknowledgement or review requirement. **View Skipped Pages** and **Restore Page** remain available. Unresolved staff assignments and unreadable pages remain visible; crop changes remain editable and applying parts is one undoable edit.

The September 20 workflow update passed 55 instrument-name checks, 28 deskew/cancellation checks, and 64 existing whole-score document checks. A live test on the 35-page Brahms Trio completed deskew, name picking, automatic extraction, two one-click exclusions, and addition of three parts with 131 bands each. This verifies interaction and assignment completeness; it does not replace the separate musical crop review.

Compact crops also retain small detached marks directly above connected target notation and allow more room above scanned slurs and ascenders. This improves the medium-skewed Trio scan; it does not establish complete automatic ownership of every direction or overlapping mark.

The latest preview also marks each recognized instrument name directly on the score with a green outline and readable label. Highlights remain while you pick more names and clear when you finish. **Find the printed title and composer automatically** is enabled for new source-header projects: Auto offers a source-image preview, **Adjust on Score**, and an optional **Use Printed Header** setting. The accepted crop is saved and applied with the parts in one undoable edit; existing manual headers and typed-title choices are preserved.

Instrument picking retains printed numbers such as “1. Violine” and “2. Violine”. Separate labels with the same spelling receive unique names; clicking the same label again updates its row. Drag around a label to read an exact box, or drag a green corner to resize that box. **Scale** and **Preferred System Gap** now commit once per slider drag and rebuild the preview in the background while keeping the previous preview visible.

**Scale above 1.00** now enlarges notation using verified blank space at the sides of source crops. The previous fixed 1.00 ceiling is removed; the remaining limit protects retained ink within the output page. With **Use Consistent Scale**, the widest strip sets that limit, which is shown in the Inspector. Reduce the new per-part **Side Margins** control to fill more of the page. Source crops stay unchanged, scale at or below 1.00 retains its previous geometry, and cached analysis keeps repeated adjustments fast. Scan speckles, instrument labels, copied directions and neighboring ink can limit enlargement. See [scale validation](../../Tests/extraction/scale-enlargement-review.md) for independent pixel comparisons and the tested Beethoven excerpt.

**Count and compress full-bar rests automatically** is enabled in the magic-wand setup and runs locally after **Add Parts**. Existing parts have **Find & Compress Rests**; an individual strip has **Count & Compress This Strip**. Recognized strips become numbered multi-bar rests with the printed clef, key, opening meter/directions and ending barline retained. Recognition runs in the background, supports cancellation, and applies one undoable edit. Original crops remain saved; **Restore Original Crop** recovers them.

This detector handles complete single-staff strips with clear barlines and whole-bar rests. It keeps uncertain passages, playing entries, internal changes and fermatas unchanged. It does not yet combine successive rest strips, handle grand staffs or split mixed playing/resting systems. Broad neighboring context may prevent detection. **Set Count Manually** is an optional fallback.
